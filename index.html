LimenOS

LimenOS is a local TV dashboard for browsing media and launching TV apps and games.

YouTube integration

The optional YouTube integration displays recent uploads from the channels you subscribe to. It can also verify that you authorized the intended YouTube channel during setup.

Google authorization uses the read-only youtube.readonly permission. LimenOS reads subscriptions, channel details and upload metadata for these features. It does not upload videos, post comments or change subscriptions.

Authorization is optional. The integration runs on your appliance and stores its credentials and feed cache there. You can log out through Settings → Accounts & feeds or revoke access in your Google Account.

Read the privacy policy for data access, storage and deletion details.
