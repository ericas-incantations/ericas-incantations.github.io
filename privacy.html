Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the LimenOS Google/YouTube integration and these informational web pages.

Google data accessed and its purpose

LimenOS uses YouTube API Services. With your permission, its optional subscription feed requests the read-only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly scope to access:

  • Your subscription list and channel identifiers, to find the channels you follow.
  • Recent upload metadata, including video identifiers, titles, channel names, publication times and thumbnail URLs, to display the feed and open selected videos in YouTube.
  • Your YouTube channel profile details, such as its identifier, title and handle, when optional channel verification is used during setup. Setup can show the verified channel name and identifier to the person configuring the appliance.

Google’s device sign-in flow supplies authorization tokens. The feed integration does not request your Google password or permission to change your subscriptions, upload videos or post comments.

Use and sharing

Google data is used for the subscription feed and account verification described above. Requests go directly from the appliance to Google/YouTube services over HTTPS. No LimenOS-operated cloud service receives your feed data or OAuth tokens.

LimenOS does not sell Google data or use it for advertising, analytics or profiling. Its use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Local storage and retention

Saved refresh tokens are encrypted on the appliance using TPM2-backed systemd-creds storage. Access tokens are held in application memory. Feed metadata is stored in a local, unencrypted JSON cache; the cache includes thumbnail URLs, not downloaded video or audio files.

Cached feed data can remain between sessions until it is refreshed or you log out. Access to the appliance, its local account and its backups should be restricted to people you trust.

Logging out and deleting data

Choose Log out for the YouTube subscription feed in Settings → Accounts & feeds to clear its active local authorization and account cache. LimenOS also attempts to revoke the Google authorization; if the appliance is offline, local logout still applies. OAuth client configuration is retained so you can authorize again.

You can also revoke access through Google Account permissions. Logging out in LimenOS additionally clears its local feed cache. Copies in device backups or filesystem snapshots may remain until those backups are removed.

YouTube playback and Google’s services

YouTube TV playback uses a separate browser session. Google/YouTube may use cookies and display advertising in that session under their own policies. Logging out of the native subscription feed does not log out of the browser account.

Google’s handling of authorization, API and playback requests is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Use of the YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

These web pages

The page code uses no cookies, analytics scripts, forms or third-party embeds. These pages do not receive OAuth tokens or YouTube feed data.

When these pages are served by GitHub Pages, GitHub handles website requests and records visitor IP addresses for security. That hosting activity is separate from the LimenOS application and is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.
